# Weekend-shift tests: results

Registered in `docs/preregistrations/3_weekend_shift.md` before any OMI value for these cities was downloaded. "Not shown" means not significant; it is not evidence against.

| Test | Day that should rise: before -> after | Day that should fall: before -> after | Shift D | p (Holm) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UAE: Friday-Saturday weekend became Saturday-Sunday (Friday a half day), 1 Jan 2022 | Fri: 0.76 -> 0.95 | Sun: 0.95 -> 0.76 | +0.384 | 5e-05 (0.0002) | **confirmed** |
| Saudi Arabia: Thursday-Friday weekend became Friday-Saturday, 29 Jun 2013 | Thu: 0.96 -> 0.99 | Sat: 0.98 -> 0.93 | +0.075 | 0.01 (0.021) | **confirmed** |
| Oman: Thursday-Friday weekend became Friday-Saturday, 1 May 2013 | Thu: 0.96 -> 1.02 | Sat: 1.03 -> 0.93 | +0.152 | 0.0001 (0.0003) | **confirmed** |
| Algeria: Thursday-Friday weekend became Friday-Saturday, 14 Aug 2009 | Thu: 0.94 -> 1.03 | Sat: 0.94 -> 0.92 | +0.113 | 0.12 (0.12) | **not shown** |

Values are NO2 on that weekday relative to working days (1.00 = a normal working day), mean over the test's cities.

**Placebos** (same test in cities whose weekend did not change): 2 of 12 have p < 0.05 (SAUDI_2013:Kuwait City, OMAN_2013:Kuwait City).
