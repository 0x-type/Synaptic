# Writing a report NASA scientists will trust

- Lead with the observation and its size in physical units (e.g. "-0.06 root-zone wetness, -14% of
  the 1981-2000 mean, over 9 cells around 31.5N 7.5W, abrupt in 1999").
- Provenance: the path the agents took (which sweep, family id, filters passed, deep dive, follow-ups).
- Checks that tried to kill it, each with its result; list explicitly the checks NOT run.
- Separate observation, interpretation, and hypothesis. Hypotheses must be testable.
- Next steps name concrete datasets (GPM IMERG, GRACE-FO, SMAP, MODIS) and analyses.
- Figures: the raw series with break/trend, the field map, and imagery if relevant.
- No hype. "Candidate signal for expert review" is the correct framing.
